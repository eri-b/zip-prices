ABOUT THE NUMBERS

How to read this data

What counts as a move?

The IRS matches addresses on returns filed in consecutive years. “People” counts individuals represented on those returns; it is not a complete population count.

What is income?

Aggregate adjusted gross income is the sum reported on destination-year returns. It is not wealth or a median income.

Missing routes and year changes

The IRS does not publish county pairs with fewer than 20 returns and may suppress other cells for privacy. A dash does not mean zero moves. Its matching process changed in 2022–2023, so that year is not directly comparable with earlier years. Connecticut changed to planning regions and is excluded.
