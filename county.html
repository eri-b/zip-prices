ABOUT THE NUMBERS

How to read this data

A move in IRS data

The IRS matches taxpayer addresses across two filing years. Returns approximate households; its n2 count represents individuals on those returns. These are tax-data measures, not a full population count.

Adjusted gross income

AGI is aggregate income on the destination-year returns, shown here in dollars. It is not wealth. AGI per return, where shown, is a mean rather than a median.

Coverage

Only the selected NY and NJ counties are included. Connecticut’s latest IRS county file uses planning regions, so Fairfield County cannot be shown exactly. Small or protected county flows may be unpublished.
