Loading ZIP boundaries and prices…Values are estimates for all homes, not averages of closed sales.

These are not recorded sale prices. Free public sale records do not consistently include bedrooms across the NYC metro. The map uses Zillow’s bedroom-specific ZIP Home Value Index (ZHVI), an estimate of the typical value of homes, including homes that did not sell.

Each displayed figure averages the available monthly ZHVI values over the selected 12, 24, or 36 months. ZIPs with fewer than 75% of months reported are left blank. Zillow reports 4-bedroom and 5+ bedroom homes separately; combining them would require unpublished counts.

Outlines are 2020 Census ZCTAs, approximations of postal ZIPs. Source: Zillow Research housing data. Data provided by Zillow Group. Processing details ↗
